# Reviewer 2 Questions and Sample Responses

Use this as a draft response sheet when preparing your paper revision letter.

## Question 1: Novelty and Contribution

**Reviewer 2 comment:**  
The contribution appears incremental. Please clarify what is new compared to prior work.

**Sample response:**  
Thank you for this helpful comment. We clarified the novelty in the Introduction and Contribution sections by explicitly distinguishing our work from prior studies. In the revised manuscript, we now state the unique methodological contribution and provide a side-by-side comparison with the most relevant references.

## Question 2: Methodology Clarity

**Reviewer 2 comment:**  
The methods section is hard to follow. Please provide clearer procedural details.

**Sample response:**  
We appreciate this feedback. We revised the Methods section to improve clarity by adding a step-by-step workflow, clearer variable definitions, and transition sentences between subsections. These changes make the procedure reproducible and easier to follow.

## Question 3: Evidence and Results

**Reviewer 2 comment:**  
The conclusions seem stronger than what the results support.

**Sample response:**  
Thank you for pointing this out. We adjusted the conclusion language to align with the presented evidence and added limitations to avoid overstatement. We also expanded the Results discussion to explain how each claim is supported by the reported findings.

## Question 4: References and Positioning

**Reviewer 2 comment:**  
Several key related papers are missing.

**Sample response:**  
We agree and have added the suggested references, along with additional recent work, in the Related Work section. We updated the discussion to better position our paper within the current literature.

## Question 5: Language and Presentation

**Reviewer 2 comment:**  
The manuscript needs language polishing.

**Sample response:**  
Thank you. We completed a full language and style edit, corrected grammar and wording issues, and improved readability throughout the manuscript.
